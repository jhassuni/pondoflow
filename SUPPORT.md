# Support

**Email:** [support@sigbin.net](mailto:support@sigbin.net) — we aim to reply within **3 working days**.

- **Found a bug?** Please use the [Bug report form](../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) — it asks for the
  steps, your phone model and the app version, which lets us fix it faster. Never post real amounts, names or
  account numbers; use made-up examples.
- **Have an idea?** Use the [Feature idea form](../../issues/new?template=feature_idea.yml).
- **Security problem?** Report it privately — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **How do I…?** The [User guide](docs/GUIDE.md) and [FAQ](docs/FAQ.md) answer the common questions.

Pondo Flow keeps your books only on your phone, so we cannot see or recover your data — please keep a backup
(Settings › Backup and restore).
